Essential guidance

Get safe first, then get help.

Fire

Leave using stairs, stay low in smoke, activate the alarm and never re-enter a burning building.

Medical emergency

Call local emergency services, give your exact location and follow the operator's instructions.

Severe weather

Move indoors away from windows, follow official alerts and avoid floodwater and downed wires.
