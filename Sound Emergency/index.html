✅ Safety Checklist

Fire emergency

  • Stop, drop, and evacuate
  • Do not use elevators
  • Move to a safe assembly point

Medical emergency

  • Call for help immediately
  • Keep the person calm
  • Follow first aid guidance

Severe weather

  • Move to shelter
  • Avoid flooded roads
  • Listen for official updates

Road emergency

  • Stay away from danger
  • Call emergency services
  • Use warning lights if safe
