Know the basics

Remember: get safe first, then get help.

FIRE

Move. Alert. Evacuate.

Leave using stairs, stay low if there is smoke, and never re-enter a burning building.

MEDICAL

Call and stay calm.

Contact local emergency services, give your location, and follow the operator’s instructions.

WEATHER

Seek proper shelter.

Move indoors away from windows and follow official alerts. Avoid floodwater and downed wires.
